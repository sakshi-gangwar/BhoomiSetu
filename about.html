Our Project

Making Land Records Smarter

BhoomiSetu is designed to provide a digital platform for managing and validating land records. It brings document processing, record validation, digital mapping and land calculations together in one place.

The system helps organize important land information such as owner details, Khasra numbers, land area and location information, while maintaining a digital record of validation results.
